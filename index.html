How it works

Touch

One finger rotates, a pinch zooms, two fingers pan. Tap a muscle to select it. Hold to press into it, and it springs back when you let go. Double-tap to fly in.

Layers

Show muscles, bones, or both. X-ray fades the muscles so the skeleton shows through. Peel away removes one muscle so you can see the layer underneath. Restore puts everything back.

Quiz

Tap any muscle or press Next muscle, then type its name. Left and right don't matter and small typos are fine. Hints cost points. Turn on Heads & parts to name each head separately, such as the long head of biceps.

Hand control

Uses the front camera to follow your hand. Open palm rotates, pinch selects, fist zooms. The video is processed on this device and never uploaded.

What's in the model

207 skeletal muscles on each side, 49 of them split into their heads or parts, and every bone of the adult skeleton, plus cartilage, intervertebral discs and teeth. Some meshes hold a whole set of small muscles, such as the eleven external intercostals or the four lumbricals of the hand. The adult body has roughly 600 skeletal muscles in total. Most of the ones missing here are tiny: the middle-ear, auricular, soft-palate, intrinsic tongue and perineal muscles.
