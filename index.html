How to use

Touch

Drag with one finger to rotate, pinch to zoom, drag with two fingers to pan. Tap a muscle to poke it, hold to press in, double-tap to fly to it.

Layers

Toggle Muscles and Bones on the left. X-ray ghosts the muscles so the skeleton shows through. Peel away removes one muscle to expose the deeper layer.

Quiz

Switch to Quiz, tap any muscle (or press Next muscle) and type its name. Hints cost points. Left/right doesn't matter.

Gesture mode

Uses the front camera to track your hand: open palm rotates, pinch selects, fist zooms. Video stays on your device.
